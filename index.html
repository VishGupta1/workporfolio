The 8 AM campaign digest

Posted every weekday from my outbound plays. It tells campaign managers which campaigns are performing and which aren't, recommends what to change, and is themed by time of year.

Recommendations for this week
  1. Front-load sends to Mon–Tue morning. Pause sequences Wed afternoon through Fri; holiday-week opens drop sharply.
  2. Clone the Year-End Budget subject line into Retail Q1 Planning. It's beating the current one 2:1.
  3. Clean the FinServ Compliance list before Monday; bounces are creeping up and hurting deliverability.
  4. Queue a light-touch "grateful for your time" follow-up for warm leads to land Monday, Dec 1.

What it does

  1. Pulls yesterday's outbound results per campaign.
  2. Compares each campaign to its own baseline and to peers.
  3. Generates specific, seasonal recommendations.
  4. Posts to the channel before managers start their day.
